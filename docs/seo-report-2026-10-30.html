Indexing has grown steadily: from 1 page in June to 76 pages by 23 Oct. Impressions averaged 230 a day from 22 September, up from 132 before, then eased back to about 158 a day in the second half of October; the new pages and speed work are aimed at lifting that baseline. All the errors the website itself caused (missing pages, redirect and "noindex" problems) are now cleared in Search Console. What remains is 47 pages Google knows about but has not indexed yet: 37 "discovered, currently not indexed", 5 where Google picked a different page as the main version, and 5 normal alternate URLs.

2. What was holding the site back

  • Google saw empty pages. The site was built so that page content only appeared after the browser ran its JavaScript. The HTML Google first downloads had no main heading and almost no text, so every page looked alike. Google delays such pages (the 37 "discovered, not indexed") and sometimes treats them as duplicates of one another.
  • The site was very slow on phones. Mobile speed score 6/100. A full-screen loading animation hid the page for up to 8 seconds, about 1.2 MB of icon fonts and a 2.4 MB image were downloaded on every visit, and content jumped around as it loaded (layout shift 0.901; Google's target is under 0.1).
  • No page targeted "builders in Chennai". The area articles were written as guides, with no single page tying the city together and nothing linking to them from the menu or footer.
  • Google did not know the business details. Offices, phone, opening hours and service area were not described in a form search engines read.

3. What we did

Google can now read every page

  • Every page (124 in total) is now generated in advance as complete HTML: heading, full text, links and business details are in the page Google downloads. The home page went from no heading and almost no text to about 3,900 words of readable content.
  • An automatic check runs on every release and stops it if any page is missing its title, main link (canonical), heading or structured data.
  • Fixed broken and dead links (logo, breadcrumbs, "Know More" and contact buttons), the duplicate /home address, and editor notes that were leaking into page source.

Chennai location pages

  • New city-wide page, hunterproperty.in/builders-in-chennai, aimed at "builders in Chennai", "top construction company in Chennai" and "builders near me". It covers costs per package, the process, plan approvals, all four offices, common questions, and links to every area page.
  • 22 new area pages (21,950 words of original content, 130 local questions answered), each written about what genuinely matters when building in that area: flooding, soil, approvals, plot sizes, coastal rules and so on.
  • 16 existing area pages upgraded with search-friendly titles and descriptions and an "area at a glance" panel: pincodes, approving authority, cost from ₹2,199 per sq ft, the office that serves the area, and links to neighbouring areas.
  • 14 other pages retitled so they stop competing with each other or with the new city page (the pages Google was confusing as duplicates).
  • Every page now links to the city page and the main services from the footer and the Services menu.

Business details for Google

  • Every page now tells search engines, in Google's structured-data format, that Hunter Property is a general contractor in Chennai with its head office in Perambur, branches in Sembiam, Royapettah and Kolathur, phone, email, opening hours, price range and social profiles.

4. When to expect results

Search rankings move on Google's schedule, not ours, and no one can honestly guarantee a position. Based on how sites like this usually respond:

5. What we need from Hunter Property

These steps move the "near me" and city-wide rankings more than anything on the website itself.
  1. Google Business Profile (one per office, if each is staffed): the name, address and phone must match the website exactly; primary category "Construction company", plus "General contractor" and "Home builder"; add services with prices, the areas served, opening hours and the website link; upload real project photos every week; post an update every week.
  2. Reviews: ask every handed-over client for a Google review and reply to each one. A steady flow of reviews is the strongest local ranking signal.
  3. Listings: the same name, address and phone on Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Houzz, Facebook and LinkedIn.
  4. Check the new pages: we kept to well-known public facts and our published prices, but someone who builds in these areas should read each page once. Real project photos and addresses for each area will make these pages much stronger.
  5. Send the Google Business Profile link so we can connect it to the website.

6. Next 90 days

  • Weeks 1–2: release, submit the sitemap, request indexing for the city page and the top area pages, and confirm the fixes in Search Console.
  • Weeks 3–6: track indexing and the first area rankings; add project photos to the area pages; tune titles based on which searches Google shows us for.
  • Weeks 7–12: project case studies (one per area where we have built), more local pages where Search Console shows demand, and a monthly report.
