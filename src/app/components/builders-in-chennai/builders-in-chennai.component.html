How we build your house

  1. Site visit and consultation. An engineer visits your plot, checks access, road level and drainage, and talks through your requirements.
  2. Document and plot check. We review the title, patta and layout approval before any design work. Our property legal team can help with verification.
  3. Design and estimate. Floor plans, elevation and Vastu-compliant layouts, with an itemised estimate on your chosen package.
  4. Plan approval. We file with the authority that covers your plot and follow it through to the permit.
  5. Construction. Soil test, foundation, structure, masonry, plastering, waterproofing and finishes, with an engineer's check at every stage.
  6. Handover. Final inspection with you, a snag list closed before handover, and the documents you need for utility connections.

Who approves a house plan in Chennai

The authority depends on where the plot is and how big the building is. Inside city limits most individual houses are approved by the Greater Chennai Corporation. Tambaram and Avadi have their own city municipal corporations. Larger buildings need approval from CMDA, and plots outside the metropolitan area fall under DTCP. Our CMDA approval guide explains the process step by step.
